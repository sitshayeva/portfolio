# 🎬 Rotten Tomatoes Movie Ratings Analysis (2010–Present)

# 🎬  Streamlit Application: https://huggingface.co/spaces/lenaras/movies

## 📁 Project Overview
This project analyzes a dataset of movies from Rotten Tomatoes, focusing on films released from 2010 onward with over 100,000 audience reviews. The goal is to explore audience and critic rating trends, genre-based preferences, and other fun movie insights for podcast listeners and film enthusiasts.

---

## 📊 Dataset Highlights

- **Total Records Analyzed**: 316 (with >100,000 audience ratings)
- **Time Frame**: 2010–Present
- **Data Source**: Maven’s Data Playground — Rotten Tomatoes dataset

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🎭 Movie Ratings Distribution

| Rating | Count | Avg Audience Score |
|--------|-------|--------------------|
| PG-13  | 160   | 67.3               |
| R      | 100   | 63.0               |
| PG     | 51    | 66.8               |
| G      | 5     | 66.2               |

> **PG-13** movies dominate in quantity and score.

---

### 📈 Audience vs Critic Ratings by Genre

| Genre             | Critics | Audience |
|------------------|---------|----------|
| 🎨 Animation      | 75.3%   | 75.2%    |
| 🧨 Action & Adv. | 60.7%   | 66.2%    |
| 🤣 Comedy         | 55.6%   | 62.8%    |

> **Animation** is universally beloved.  
> **Comedies** underperform on both fronts.

---

### 🧠 Ratings Over Time

- Ratings stayed **stable** across the decade
- Small dip around **2013–2014**

---

### ⏱️ Runtime vs Rating

- Longer movies attract more critic reviews
- No strong link between runtime and rating
- Diverse audience preferences!

---

### 🌟 Top 5 Most Reviewed Movies

| Title               | Audience Reviews |
|--------------------|------------------|
| Shutter Island      | 2.37M            |
| The Dark Knight Rises | 1.21M          |
| Avengers (2012)     | 1.13M            |

> **Shutter Island** stands out for massive audience engagement.
